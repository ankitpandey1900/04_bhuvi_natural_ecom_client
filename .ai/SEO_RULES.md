# SEO Rules

## Standards

- Public pages must use metadata and canonical strategy.
- Use structured data where appropriate.
- Preserve sitemap and robots outputs for public content.
- Keep content and product pages crawlable and indexable.

## Required Practices

- Do not introduce public routes without considering SEO impact.
- Ensure metadata is generated from shared logic where possible.
- Keep content and storefront pages performant and crawlable.
